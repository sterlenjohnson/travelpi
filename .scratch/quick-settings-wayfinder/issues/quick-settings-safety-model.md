## Question

Which settings belong in the compact page and how should they apply?

## Resolution

Expose status and narrow controls for AdGuard, Unbound, optional encrypted DNS, IPv6, SQM, and current WAN identity. Stage DNS, IPv6, SQM, and identity changes; confirm service restarts. Keep full AdGuard administration in its own GUI.
